    About the course

    Red Horizon

    In this interactive game, learn how greenhouse engineers balance temperature, light, water, and energy to keep crops healthy in a Mars habitat.

    STEM Topic and Branch

    STEM Topic Greenhouse environment control and resource management
    Branch of STEM Engineering, supported by plant and environmental science

    How the game supports learning

    Students adjust temperature, light, water, and energy controls, then observe how one change affects the others. Immediate feedback and an evidence notebook guide them from noticing a result to explaining the science.

    WarmthPlants need a suitable temperature.
    LightPlants need light for growth.
    WaterWater can be conserved and recycled.
    EnergyEvery control uses one shared battery.

    Learning Outcomes

    AUDIENCE BEHAVIOUR CONDITION DEGREE

    Given limited battery and water, Secondary 4–6 students (Grades 10–12, ages 15–18) will adjust greenhouse heating, lighting, and water systems to keep canopy temperature between 18°C and 26°C and crop health at or above 60%.

    Given sensor readings and action results, Secondary 4–6 students (Grades 10–12, ages 15–18) will correctly explain at least two trade-offs among temperature, light, water, and energy, using at least two specific pieces of evidence.

    Six linked challenges · 15–20 minutes · Immediate feedback · Evidence-based debrief
